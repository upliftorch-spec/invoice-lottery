FAQ

What is the Taiwan receipt lottery?

Every official receipt (uniform invoice, 統一發票) in Taiwan doubles as a lottery ticket. Draws happen every two months and the top prize is NT$10 million — the government's way of encouraging consumers to ask for receipts.

When are the draws?

On the 25th of every odd month (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov). For example, receipts dated January–February are drawn on March 25.

How do I claim a prize?

Prizes of NT$200–1,000 can be redeemed at major convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart, etc.). Larger prizes are claimed at designated banks with your receipt and ID. You have 3 months starting from the 6th of the month after the draw.
